Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

Strandard values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Strandard mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Strandard, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials (securely managed through our authentication provider)
  • Content you choose to submit (such as reviews, videos, captions, or brand suggestions)
  • Optional profile information you choose to provide

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Strandard, certain information is collected automatically to help us operate and improve the app. This may include:

  • App usage data (such as interactions within the app, screens viewed, and search activity)
  • Device information (such as device type, operating system, and app version)
  • Unique device identifiers and session identifiers
  • Diagnostic information, crash logs, and performance data

We use analytics tools, including Firebase Analytics and UXCam, to understand how users interact with the app and to improve functionality, stability, and user experience. These tools may collect device identifiers, usage events, and session data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Strandard does not collect contacts, precise location data, or sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Strandard account
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Allow you to browse brands, reviews, and videos
  • Enable user generated content submissions
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Analyze feature usage and app interactions
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Communicate essential account related messages (such as verification or password resets)

We do not sell your personal data.

3. User Generated Content

If you submit reviews, videos, or other content:

  • That content may be visible to other users within the app
  • You are responsible for the content you choose to share
  • Content you submit may be processed by analytics tools for the purpose of improving app functionality and monitoring app performance
  • You may request removal of your content by deleting your account or contacting us

4. Analytics and Session Monitoring

To improve Strandard’s reliability and user experience, we use analytics and session monitoring tools. These tools help us:

  • Understand how features are used
  • Identify technical issues
  • Diagnose crashes
  • Improve performance and navigation

These tools may collect device identifiers, usage data, and screen interaction information. Data collected through analytics tools is used strictly for app functionality, performance improvement, and security purposes. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Strandard uses trusted third party infrastructure to securely store and process data, including authentication services, cloud databases, file storage, and analytics platforms.

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You may delete your account at any time directly within the app.

When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication credentials are removed
  • Associated personal data is deleted or anonymized where applicable
  • User generated content may be removed or anonymized depending on your deletion selection

If you need assistance, you may contact us at support@thestrandard.com.

7. Third Party Services

Strandard relies on third party services to operate the app, including backend infrastructure providers and analytics services. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their functionality and are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Strandard is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated Last updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you may contact us at:

Email: support@thestrandard.com
