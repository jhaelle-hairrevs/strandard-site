Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 27, 2026

Strandard values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Strandard mobile application and related services.

1. Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Strandard, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials (securely managed through our authentication provider)
  • Content you choose to submit (such as reviews, videos, or brand suggestions)

2. Information Collected Automatically

We may collect limited technical information, including:

  • App usage data (such as interactions within the app)
  • Device and session information necessary to operate the app reliably
  • We do not collect contacts, precise location data, or sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Strandard account.
  • Allow you to browse brands, reviews, and videos
  • Enable user-generated content submissions
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Communicate essential account-related messages (such as verification or password resets)

We do not sell your personal data.

4. User-Generated Content

If you submit reviews, videos, or other content:

  • That content may be visible to other users
  • You are responsible for the content you choose to share
  • You may request removal of your content by deleting your account or contacting us.

5. Data Storage & Security

Strandard uses trusted third-party infrastructure to securely store and process data, including:

  • Authentication services
  • Cloud databases and file storage

We take reasonable measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Account Deletion & Data Removal

You may delete your account at any time directly within the app.

When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication credentials are removed
  • Your associated personal data is deleted or anonymized where applicable

If you need assistance, you may also contact us at: support@thestrandard.com

7. Third-Party Services

Strandard may rely on third-party services to operate the app (such as backend infrastructure and analytics). These services process data only as needed to provide their functionality and are subject to their own privacy practices.

8. Children’s Privacy

Strandard is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated “Last updated” date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you may contact us at:

Email: support@thestrandard.com
