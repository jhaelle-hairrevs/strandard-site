Privacy Policy

Here’s how Strandard collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Strandard mobile application and related services.

Last updated: February 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Strandard, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials (securely managed through our authentication provider)
  • Content you choose to submit (such as reviews, videos, captions, or brand suggestions)
  • Optional profile information you choose to provide

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Strandard, certain information is collected automatically to help us operate and improve the app. This may include:

  • App usage data (such as interactions within the app, screens viewed, and search activity)
  • Device information (such as device type, operating system, and app version)
  • Unique device identifiers and session identifiers
  • Diagnostic information, crash logs, and performance data

We use analytics tools, including Firebase Analytics and UXCam, to understand how users interact with the app and to improve functionality, stability, and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Strandard account
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Allow you to browse brands, reviews, and videos
  • Enable user generated content submissions
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Analyze feature usage and interactions
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Communicate essential account related messages

3. User Generated Content

Content you submit may be visible to other users. You may request removal by deleting your account or contacting us.

4. Analytics and Session Monitoring

Analytics and monitoring tools help us understand feature usage, identify issues, and improve performance. Data is used strictly for functionality and security purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Strandard uses trusted third party infrastructure to securely store and process data. While we implement safeguards, no system is completely secure.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at support@thestrandard.com.
