Delete Your Strandard Account

If you’d like to permanently delete your account and associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request below.

Note: If you no longer have access to the email on your account, contact support@thestrandard.com for help.

What gets deleted

When your deletion is processed, we remove:

  • Your account authentication credentials
  • Your saved profile details (hair profile, preferences)
  • Your submitted personal data that is tied to your account
  • Any private data linked to your email or user ID

What may remain

If you posted reviews or content that appears publicly, we will either delete it or anonymize it so it is no longer tied to you, depending on what’s required for platform integrity and legal compliance.

How long it takes

Most deletion requests are processed within 7 days. In some cases, it may take up to 30 days to fully remove data from backups.
